Why it is different

Every turn changes the cut

01 · Turn

The face decides

Only the stone nearest the rail can be struck. Turn the table 90° and a different face meets the chisel.

02 · Rack

Plan the hooks

A chisel with power left hangs on a hook. Fill every hook and the level stops, so the order matters.

03 · Calm

90 statues, no rush

Three chapters, from The Yard to The Gallery. No timers, no lives, no energy bar.

How to play

Three moves, many choices

  1. Tap a chisel. It runs along the rail and carves the front stone of its colour in each column.
  2. Turn the turntable to bring another face down to the rail.
  3. Keep a hook free. Unveil the statue in as few turns as you can for three stars.

Tools when you are stuck: Extra Hook, Pick and Mallet. The Yard (30 statues) is free; All Chapters opens the other 60 with one purchase.
