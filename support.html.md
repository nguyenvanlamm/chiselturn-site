## How to play
Tap a chisel: it runs along the rail under the block and carves the stone nearest the rail in each column, if that stone has the chisel's colour. Turn the table with ↺ and ↻ to bring another face down to the rail. A chisel with power left hangs on a hook; when every hook is taken, the level stops. Extra Hook adds a hook (also after the rack fills), Pick lets you use a chisel from a back row, and Mallet breaks every stone of one colour that faces the rail.

## Purchases
The Yard (30 statues) is free. All Chapters opens The Quarry and The Gallery (60 more) with one purchase. Tool Pouch and Tool Chest add Extra Hooks, Picks and Mallets. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open *Settings → Restore purchases*.

## Progress
Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
