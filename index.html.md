# Chiselturn, a calm turntable puzzle for iPhone. Turn the stone. Carve the statue.

**Coming soon to the App Store.**

A block of coloured stone stands on a turntable above one rail. Choose the face, choose the chisel, and watch a picture come out of the rock.

## Every turn changes the cut

- **The face decides.** Only the stone nearest the rail can be struck. Turn the table 90° and a different face meets the chisel.
- **Plan the hooks.** A chisel with power left hangs on a hook. Fill every hook and the level stops, so the order matters.
- **90 statues, no rush.** Three chapters, from The Yard to The Gallery. No timers, no lives, no energy bar.

## How it plays

1. **Tap a chisel.** It runs along the rail and carves the front stone of its colour in each column.
2. **Turn the turntable** to bring another face down to the rail.
3. **Keep a hook free.** Unveil the statue in as few turns as you can for three stars.

Tools when you are stuck: Extra Hook, Pick and Mallet. The Yard (30 statues) is included; All Chapters opens the other 60 with one purchase.
