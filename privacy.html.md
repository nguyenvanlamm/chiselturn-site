# Privacy Policy
Chiselturn · last updated 31 October 2026

Chiselturn is a puzzle game published by Luong Nguyen. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

## What stays on your device

- Your progress: the best stars for each statue you carved and how many Extra Hooks, Picks and Mallets you hold.
- Whether you have bought All Chapters.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

## What we do not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No advertising and no ad networks.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

## Purchases
Chapter 1, The Yard (30 statues), is free. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

## Children
The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
